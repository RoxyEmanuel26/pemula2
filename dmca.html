DMCA Takedown & 2257 Policy

Copyright Infringement, EU Notice-and-Takedown & 18 U.S.C. § 2257 Exemption

About DMCA & Copyright Compliance

kumpulenak respects the intellectual property rights of creators and studios worldwide. We operate strictly as an automated search index and video embed directory — we do not upload, host, or store video files on our servers. We respond promptly to valid copyright notices under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA, 17 U.S.C. § 512) and the EU Digital Services Act (DSA).

DMCA Claim Procedure

To submit an official takedown notice, please email our designated copyright team with the following information:

1

Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.

2

Exact URLs on www.kumpulenak.my.id pointing to the allegedly infringing listing.

3

Contact details including your full legal name, organization (if applicable), email address, and telephone number.

4

A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

5

A signed declaration (electronic signature accepted) under penalty of perjury that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

Send DMCA / Removal Request

Send copyright or removal notices directly to:

dmca@kumpulenak.my.id

Verified claims are reviewed and processed within 24–72 business hours.

18 U.S.C. § 2257 Record-Keeping Requirements Compliance Statement

kumpulenak is not a producer (primary or secondary) of any visual content found on this website. With respect to 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75, our activity is limited strictly to indexing and embedding third-party media streams hosted by external providers who maintain primary compliance records for all performers depicted. Every performer appearing in indexed material is required to be at least 18 years of age at the time of production.

We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against non-consensual or underage material. Any suspected violation reported to dmca@kumpulenak.my.id is removed immediately and permanently blocked.

Takedown Processing Workflow

  • Upon receiving a valid notice, we immediately review the reported URLs.
  • Verified infringing or unauthorized listings are removed and de-indexed within 24–72 hours.
  • The claimant receives a confirmation email once removal is complete.

Important Legal Notice

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), submitting knowingly false or bad-faith DMCA claims may result in legal liability and damages. Please verify ownership or legal authorization prior to filing a notice.
