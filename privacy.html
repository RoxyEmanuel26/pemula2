Privacy Policy

International Privacy, EU GDPR & US CCPA/CPRA Notice — Updated November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how kumpulenak ("we", "us", or "our platform") collects, uses, and protects limited technical data when you visit www.kumpulenak.my.id. Because kumpulenak requires no user registration or account creation, we minimize personal data collection by design.

Information We Collect

1. Standard Technical Telemetry

Like all web services, our edge CDN and analytics infrastructure may log non-identifying technical metadata:

  • Anonymized Network & IP Metadata — used for DDoS protection, regional edge routing, and abuse prevention.
  • Browser & Device Type — used to optimize mobile and desktop HTML5 playback.
  • Pages Visited & Referrers — aggregated to improve site performance and category curation.

2. Functional & Partner Cookies

  • Theme Preference Cookie — stores your dark/light theme choice (kumpulenak_theme).
  • Third-Party Advertising & Analytics — partners such as Adsterra, Monetag, and Google Analytics may place cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

3. Direct Support Inquiries

If you contact us via email, we process only your email address and message content to reply to your support, privacy, or DMCA inquiry.

European Union & UK Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access & Portability — request confirmation of any personal data held from direct support communications.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") — request deletion of support correspondence at any time.
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing — control analytics and advertising cookies through browser settings or opt-out mechanisms.

California & US Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. Residents of California and other US states with comprehensive privacy statutes may request disclosure or deletion of any personal data provided directly via email by contacting admin@kumpulenak.my.id.

Third-Party Infrastructure & Partners

  • Cloudflare & Vercel — global CDN delivery, TLS encryption, and edge security.
  • Adsterra & Monetag — third-party advertising networks funding free access.
  • External Video APIs — embedded HTML5 streams hosted by third-party media providers.

Managing Cookies

You can restrict or clear cookies at any time in your browser preferences:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Strict 18+ Policy & Protection of Minors

This website contains adult-oriented media and is strictly prohibited for individuals under the age of 18 (or the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Contact admin@kumpulenak.my.id immediately if any concern arises.
